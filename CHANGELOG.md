## [2.0.0] - 2026-10-21

### Summary
Orbit becomes a menu bar app with dozens of tools, merged from Vorssaint, while keeping its maintenance window: Dashboard, Energy, Cleanup, Disk Analyzer, Applications, Maintenance, Processes and History.

### Highlights
- **One app for monitoring and tools.** System monitor, alerts, network, keep awake, port manager, volume mixer, window management, clipboard history, screen capture and more now live in Orbit's menu bar panel.
- **Maintenance window kept.** Open it from the menu bar panel: everything Orbit 1.x did is still there.

### Changed
- Orbit is now licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later, because it includes code from Vorssaint (https://github.com/vorssaint/vorssaint-utils). Earlier history is in docs/upstream-vorssaint-CHANGELOG.md.
